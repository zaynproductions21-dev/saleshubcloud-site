# SalesHub Cloud logo brief

## Brand

- **What it does:** a CRM that carries a law firm's enquiry through every stage (contact → enquiry → lead → opportunity → case) to a signed retainer, with WhatsApp, email, e-signing and case notes logged against the one record.
- **For whom:** the principal or practice manager at a UK immigration or housing firm who pays per seat, and the caseworkers and fee earners who have it open all day (the Abrahams team first).
- **Must own:** *orderly*. A place where nothing gets lost: every case in its stage, every action on the file.
- **Must never suggest:** *a generic sales gadget*, the hype-SaaS look that the "GPT-4o powered" copy and the gradient headline already lean towards.
- **Where it lives, ranked:** favicon and browser tab of `app.saleshubcloud.com` (open all day) → app sidebar and site nav at ~28–32px → client documents and e-sign pages printed in mono → social avatar in a circle crop → app icon.
- **Name anatomy:** `SalesHub Cloud`. "Cloud" is a descriptor; the mark is `SalesHub` = `sales|hub`, seam at `s|h`. Two `s`s (awkward to draw), then `h u b`: three letters made only of stems and round arches, and `h`/`u` are rotations of each other. `l`, `h` and `b` give ascenders; no descenders. Monogram `SH`.
- **The mechanism as a shape:** stages a case moves through (kanban columns, a stepped path); the case file itself with its index tabs; one line carried unbroken from first contact to signature; the signature line on the retainer.
- **Competitors to stay clear of:** the name itself is made of two giants' words, so stay **far** from Salesforce's cloud and HubSpot's sprocket: **no clouds, no hubs with spokes, no orbit dots**. Legal CRMs/PMS: Clio (lowercase blue wordmark), LEAP (bold red wordmark), Lawmatics (purple), Actionstep (orange arrow), Insight Legal and Osprey (generic marks). Also avoid the legal clichés: scales, gavels, columns, quills.
- **Existing equity:** teal `#0BAEAB` (customers see it on every button) and the four concentric circles. **Keep the teal, drop the circles**: the bullseye reads as a target or a radio button and is near a dozen stock "hub" icons. `#0BAEAB` is only 2.74:1 on white, below the 3:1 a graphic needs, so the logo accent uses the site's own darker step `#099C99` (3.37:1 on white, 4.42:1 on the dark nav `#28243D`). Ink is the site text colour `#2F2B3D`.

Inference stated, not asked: "Cloud" stays out of the mark and appears only as a lighter descriptor in the lockup.

## Concepts considered (16 → 4)

1. Concentric target (the current mark): **killed**, generic bullseye and radio button.
2. Hub with spokes or orbit dots: killed, HubSpot.
3. A cloud: killed, Salesforce and every hosting firm.
4. Scales of justice: killed, legal cliché, and the product is not a law firm.
5. Gavel: killed, courts not intake; cliché.
6. Sales funnel: killed, generic sales software.
7. Rising bar chart or arrow: killed, signal-bar AI tell and generic growth.
8. Handshake: killed, stock icon.
9. Speech bubble for the WhatsApp feed: killed, stock icon and reads as a chat app.
10. **One line through `hub`.** A constructed monoline wordmark in which `h`, `u` and `b` share their strokes: the `h` leg is the `u`'s left side and the `u`'s right side rises into the `b` stem. One unbroken line, like a case carried from enquiry to retainer. **Kept (A).**
11. **Joined H.** An `H` whose crossbar is an S-curve stepping from the left stem down to the right: `SH` in one form, the two sides (firm and client) joined. **Kept (B).**
12. **The bundle.** A case file with three index tabs down its edge, the last one filled: the stages of a matter, in the object every caseworker handles. **Kept (C).**
13. **Signed.** A serif wordmark with a flat accent rule beneath it, the signature line of the retainer the whole pipeline exists to reach. The letterhead voice. **Kept (D).**
14. A seal or stamp emblem with `SH`: killed, illegible at 16px and text in a symbol.
15. Kanban columns as three bars: killed, indistinguishable from signal bars.
16. Square brackets around a dot ("everything in one place"): killed, reads as a developer tool.

## Chosen: 08 Heavy S, with the mark as the S of the name (user's pick, 2026-11-07)

Variants are in `refine/` (`build.py` outlines the rest of the name from Archivo 800/400, OFL, in `fonts/`; comparison in `refine/sheet.png`). The S is built from the same geometry as the icon. It is set to Archivo's cap height (687 units) and its own S box (left bearing 44, width 613), with the stroke at 0.213 × cap, so its weight matches the type's horizontals.

- **08a · Cap height:** the S sits exactly on the caps, followed by "alesHub" at 800 and "Cloud" at 400. **Recommended for the site and documents.** It reads as one word, and the teal lower half of the S is the only colour.
- **08b · No "Cloud":** the same wordmark without the descriptor. **Recommended for the app sidebar and anywhere under ~24px tall.**
- **08c · Raised:** the S at 1.28 × cap height, like a drop cap. It is louder, but at nav size it looks like a separate initial rather than part of the word.
- **Rule:** never put the square icon next to the wordmark. "S SalesHub" doubles the S, as the nav cells on the sheet show. The icon is used alone (favicon, app icon, avatar), and the wordmark is used alone everywhere else.

## Final: 08a (user's pick, 2026-11-07)

Built by `final/build.py` (see `final/usage.md`). Wired into all 19 pages with `python final/build.py --install`.
