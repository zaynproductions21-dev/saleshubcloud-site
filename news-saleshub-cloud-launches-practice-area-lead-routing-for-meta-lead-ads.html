SalesHub Cloud Launches Practice-Area Lead Routing for Meta Lead Ads

New legal CRM UK feature sends Facebook and Instagram leads straight to the right practice team — housing, immigration, family, conveyancing or employment

11 July 2026 · SalesHub Cloud

11 July 2026, UK —

SalesHub Cloud, the SRA-aligned legal CRM built for UK law firms, has launched practice-area lead routing for Meta lead ads. Firms running Facebook and Instagram lead forms can now tag each form with the practice area it serves — housing, immigration, family, conveyancing or employment — and every enquiry lands in the right team's pipeline the moment it arrives. No more piling up in a shared inbox. No more waiting for someone to sort it.

The feature works at the form level. When a firm registers a Meta lead form, it records which practice area that form belongs to. From then on, every lead submitted through that form is identified by its origin and routed straight to the matching pipeline, where the relevant follow-up sequences kick off immediately. The routing lookup uses form metadata only — no personal client data is involved in the matching step — keeping the whole process GDPR-compliant.

"When every Meta lead drops into one pile, someone has to sort it by hand, and speed dies," said a SalesHub Cloud spokesperson. "A housing disrepair enquiry should never sit behind a stack of conveyancing quotes. Route it to the right team in the first minute and the firm answers while the client is still on their phone. That's how you convert the ad spend you're already paying for."

And that points to a real problem the launch is designed to fix. Too many law firm intake systems try to guess a lead's practice area from its form fields. It's a fragile approach — it breaks the moment a firm adds a new practice area or launches a new campaign, and misrouted enquiries go cold before anyone notices. Explicit per-form routing removes the guesswork entirely. Each form's destination is declared once and holds, no matter how many campaigns a firm runs.

Practice-area routing is available now to all SalesHub Cloud customers at no extra cost. New practice areas beyond the standard five can be added as firms need them. The platform combines pipeline visibility, GPT-4o powered intake features and SRA-aligned case tracking on UK-hosted infrastructure, and comes with a 14-day free trial — no card required.
