Manage your pipeline your way

Every lead and case gets its own place in a visual, multi-stage pipeline. Drag deals between stages, spot what's stuck, and keep the whole team working from the same source of truth.

Each record holds the full history — emails, calls, notes, files, signed documents. So when a colleague picks up an enquiry, nothing's been lost and they're up to speed in seconds.

You can tailor pipeline stages to match how your firm actually works. Whether that's an immigration matter, a housing case, or a standard B2B sales cycle — the pipeline bends to fit you, not the other way around.

AI lead scoring and risk flags

Rather than guessing which enquiries deserve attention first, SalesHub Cloud scores your leads so the team can focus on the ones most likely to convert. It's not just scoring, either. The AI surfaces case notes, flags risks, and catches stalling deals before they go cold.

The goal? Less time on admin. More time on the conversations that actually win work.

Automation and multi-channel follow-up

Following up is where most pipelines quietly fall apart. SalesHub Cloud takes the repetitive work off your plate — drip sequences, reminders, stage-based actions — across email and SMS. Set your sequence once, and the platform keeps every lead warm from there. No one has to remember the next step. It just happens.

WhatsApp, Facebook and TikTok lead integrations

Your clients message you where they're already hanging out. SalesHub Cloud connects to the WhatsApp Business API via Twilio — so you can send, receive, and log conversations directly against a contact or case. Leads from Facebook and Instagram sync automatically through the Meta Graph API. TikTok leads arrive via real-time webhook, with a scheduled fallback if you need it.

And here's the thing — every lead gets deduplicated and assigned automatically. No manual sorting. No cross-channel blind spots. Nothing slips through.

E-signing and document templates

Send client agreements and onboarding paperwork without leaving the platform. SalesHub Cloud gives you HTML document templates with merge fields, PDF generation, and OTP-verified client signatures — every step fully audit-logged. Your paperwork stays compliant. Your client file stays complete.

Security and UK data residency

SalesHub Cloud is built for UK firms and keeps all data stored in the UK. Every record — contacts, cases, emails, files — is tied to a single organisation ID. That means no data bleeds across organisations; your team works in a fully isolated environment, completely separate from anyone else on the platform.

It's GDPR-compliant, and if you need to remove someone's access in a hurry, one click disables their account instantly — blocking the login and killing all active sessions on the spot.
